A calm morning is mostly a matter of order. Work backwards from the time you need to leave for the ceremony, and leave a little breathing space.

## Start with the departure time

Decide when you need to leave, then work back through photographs, getting dressed, hair and makeup. Share the plan with your photographer and with us.

## Allow time for everyone

If bridesmaids and family are having their hair or makeup done too, say so in your enquiry. we can then suggest an order that keeps everyone on schedule.

## Build in a buffer

Things overrun, and that is fine. A cushion of time means a late delivery or a long hug never becomes a problem.

## Have your references ready

Photographs of styles you love, and your hair accessories, make the morning smoother. Bring your veil or headpiece so it can be fitted properly.
