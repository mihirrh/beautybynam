Good skin on the wedding morning is mostly about consistency. A simple routine you can keep up usually beats an ambitious one you abandon.

## Keep it simple

Cleanse, moisturise and wear sun protection every day. Drink water, and try not to change everything at once.

## Avoid trying new things close to the day

Introduce new products and treatments well in advance, so any reaction shows itself with time to settle. Avoid anything unfamiliar in the final fortnight.

## Talk to a professional if you have concerns

If your skin is sensitive, reactive or you have a specific concern, speak to a pharmacist, GP or dermatologist. This article is general advice, not medical advice.

## Tell your makeup artist

Let Nam know about allergies and sensitivities when you enquire. It helps her choose the right preparation and products for you.
