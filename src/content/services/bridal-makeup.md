Bridal makeup has to do three things at once. It has to look beautiful in person, work for the camera and last from the first photograph to the last dance.

Nam starts with your skin, then builds the look around your features. Together you decide how much definition you want in the eyes, which lip colour suits your dress and how soft or polished the finish should be.

If you would like a more complete picture before the day, mention it in your enquiry and Nam can talk you through the options.
